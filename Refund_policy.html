Refund Policy

Effective date: 6 September 2026

Current Donation Process

This website currently collects donation enquiries by email. It does not process card payments or take payment details through the website.

Refund Requests

If you have made a donation through an external payment provider or financial institution and believe a refund is needed, contact us as soon as possible at Cerebralpalsycentre@gmail.com with the date, amount, payment reference, and reason. Do not send full card numbers or passwords.

We will review requests fairly and explain the available next steps. Refunds may depend on the payment method, the relevant provider's rules, fraud checks, and applicable law. A refund cannot be guaranteed where the payment was made directly to a third party or where the payment provider controls the transaction.
