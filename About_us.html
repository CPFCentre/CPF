About Us

Who We Are

Dahlia Cerebral Palsy C.B.O is a non-profit organization registered with the Ministry of Gender and Social Development. We provide a complete rehabilitation programme and special needs education for children with various challenges.

The Centre humbly started in 1988 to cater for children with cerebral palsy as well as other neurological disorders. We were formerly known as Cerebral Palsy Foundation - Tudor.

Our multidisciplinary team works alongside parents and caregivers to provide education, assessment, therapy and practical support based on each learner's needs.

We Cater For

  • Cerebral palsy
  • Autism spectrum disorders
  • Attention deficit disorder / hyperactivity
  • Emotional and behavioural disorders
  • Sensory integration disorders
  • Communication disorders
  • Down syndrome
  • Learning difficulties
  • Delayed milestones

Education and Vocational Training

We offer special needs education through the four stage-based levels: Foundation, Intermediate, Pre-vocational and Vocational. Learners are prepared for the job market with the skills and attitude needed for employment.

Vocational courses include culinary arts, information communication technology, sign language, bead and needle work, and appropriate paper-based technology. Parents and caregivers are also trained to understand and implement programmes at home.

Co-curricular Activities

Learners take part in ball games, yoga, swimming and school trips. These activities provide experiences beyond the classroom and help build independence and employment opportunities.

Our Vision

Our institution is geared towards nurturing every learner's potential and helping learners become independent members of society.

Our Mission

To provide special education for all individuals with special needs and develop them holistically.
