Our Name, Our Story

From Cerebral Palsy Foundation - Tudor to Dahlia Cerebral Palsy C.B.O

Author: Dahlia Cerebral Palsy C.B.O  •  Date: 7 September 2026

Dahlia Cerebral Palsy Community Based Organization (C.B.O) is the new name of the organization formerly known as Cerebral Palsy Foundation - Tudor. The name has changed, but our commitment to children and learners with special needs remains at the heart of everything we do.

Honouring our history

The Centre humbly started in 1988 to cater for children with cerebral palsy and other neurological disorders. Over the years, our work has grown to include complete rehabilitation, special needs education, assessment, therapy, family support and vocational training.

Why Dahlia Cerebral Palsy C.B.O?

The new name reflects the wider community we serve. We support children and learners with cerebral palsy, autism spectrum disorders, communication disorders, developmental delays, Down syndrome, learning difficulties and other challenges. We work alongside parents and caregivers so that each learner can receive appropriate, individualized support.

Our programmes include physical, occupational, speech and cognitive stimulation therapy, education across foundation, intermediate, pre-vocational and vocational levels, and practical courses such as culinary arts, information communication technology, sign language, bead and needle work, and appropriate paper-based technology.

Same purpose, continued service

Dahlia Cerebral Palsy C.B.O will continue nurturing every learner's potential and helping learners become more independent members of society. We are grateful to the families, staff, volunteers, donors and community partners who have supported this journey and made the Centre's work possible.

Whether you knew us as Cerebral Palsy Foundation - Tudor or know us today as Dahlia Cerebral Palsy C.B.O, we welcome you to remain part of our story.
