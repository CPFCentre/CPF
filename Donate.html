Why Donate to Dahlia Cerebral Palsy C.B.O?

You can support our institution by volunteering services in your speciality, helping us reach children with special needs in the community, or donating in kind or in cash to support our programmes.

Your support helps provide rehabilitation, special needs education, therapy, vocational training and practical opportunities that prepare learners for greater independence and employment.

  • Help families access early intervention and assessment
  • Support community outreach for children with special needs
