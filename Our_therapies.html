Our Therapies

Services Offered

Before placement, learners are assessed by our special needs teachers, early childhood development education (ECDE) teachers, occupational therapists and physiotherapists. These specialists provide a case impression to place each learner at the correct level and provide appropriate individualized services.

The multidisciplinary team works alongside parents to provide therapeutic intervention based on the needs of the child and family. Services include physical, occupational, speech and cognitive stimulation therapy.

Physical Therapy

Physical therapy supports mobility, strength, coordination and functional independence for learners with physical disabilities and neurological conditions.

Occupational Therapy

Occupational therapy develops practical daily living, self-care, productivity and participation skills according to each learner's abilities and needs.

Speech Therapy

Speech therapy supports communication, language development and participation for learners with communication disorders and other developmental challenges.

Cognitive Stimulation Therapy

Cognitive stimulation activities support learning, attention, problem-solving and participation in daily activities.

Early Intervention

Early intervention can have a significant impact on a child's ability to learn new skills and overcome challenges. Our team works with families to support the learner's development holistically.
