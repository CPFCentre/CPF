This notice is intended to support transparency under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019 and related regulations. Whether the organisation must register with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner depends on its activities, scale, and processing. Confirm that position with Kenyan privacy counsel.

Information We Receive

When you send a donation enquiry, we receive the name, email address, and donation information you choose to provide. We may also receive messages you send by email, phone, WhatsApp, or social media.

The website does not require an account, collect payment-card details, or intentionally collect sensitive information through its forms. Please do not include medical records or other sensitive information in a donation enquiry.

Why We Use It

  • To respond to enquiries and discuss donations or support.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the website.
  • To keep basic records where required for accountability or legal obligations.

Sharing and Retention

We do not sell personal information. We share information only when needed to respond to you, provide a service you request, comply with law, protect people or property, or operate the website through a service provider. We keep information only for as long as reasonably needed for these purposes.

Your Choices

You may ask what personal information we hold about you, request correction, or ask us to delete it where the law allows. You may withdraw consent for future processing by contacting us, although this does not affect processing already carried out lawfully.

Children and Safeguarding

This website is not intended to collect personal information directly from children. Parents, guardians, or authorised representatives should contact us about any child-related enquiry.

Changes

We may update this policy when our services, technology, or legal obligations change. The effective date above will show when it was last reviewed.
